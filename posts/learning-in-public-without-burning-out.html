Learning in public without burning out

A sustainable writing habit is smaller than you think. Here is the routine that kept me publishing for years.

Jul 21, 2026 · 5 min read

I used to believe every post had to be a masterpiece. That belief kept me silent for months.

Small, regular, honest

Now I write one short note a week, about something I learned that same week. It takes an hour, and it never needs to be perfect.

Consistency beats intensity when the work is meant to last.
